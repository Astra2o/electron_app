Circle to Lens

Press Alt+Shift+S anywhere on Windows, draw a circle around anything, and search it with Google Lens — straight to your default browser.

How to use

  1. 1

    Press the hotkey

    Alt+Shift+S from any app. Circle to Lens stays running in your system tray. Change the combo in Settings.

  2. 2

    Draw a circle

    Around anything on screen — text, an image, an object. Pick your pencil style in Settings.

  3. 3

    Lens opens in your browser

    The image is uploaded from your own browser session, so results render with your Google account — no "image not found" errors.
